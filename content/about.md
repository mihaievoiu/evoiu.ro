I've been making photographs for over a decade — first out of curiosity, then obsession, and finally as the clearest way I know to pay attention to the world.

My work is drawn toward the quiet and the overlooked: the particular quality of afternoon light on a concrete wall, the way a stranger's posture tells a whole story, the geometry that emerges when you look at familiar spaces from an unfamiliar angle.

I believe a good photograph doesn't explain — it invites. It creates a space for the viewer to bring something of themselves.

When I'm not shooting, I'm reading about the history of photography, wandering cities I don't know, and drinking too much coffee.

For commissions, collaborations, or just to talk photography, reach me at [mihai.evoiu@gmail.com](mailto:mihai.evoiu@gmail.com).
