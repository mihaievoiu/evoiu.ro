## Dear visitor,

I'm Mihai. I photograph how I interact with the world. What I came to realise is that beyond photons, light can be a very personal thing. For me it was always more about the trace left or the dust speck rather than the beam itself. The selections here, either close-by or from far away, are not meant to be short or efficient, they are more of a travel journal filled with personal notes.
